TENDERS & CONTRACT WORK

Give us a contract for your furniture needs

We take on bulk orders and tenders for builders, schools, offices, hostels, and government projects — from custom manufacturing to full on-site fit-outs.

How a tender works with us

A simple, direct process — no middlemen, no delays.

1

Share requirements

Send us your tender document, quantity, and specifications through the form below or by phone.

2

Site visit & quote

We assess the scope, visit the site if needed, and share a detailed quotation.

3

Agreement & timeline

Once approved, we finalise the contract, materials, and delivery schedule.

4

Manufacture & deliver

Furniture is built in our workshop and delivered or installed on-site.
